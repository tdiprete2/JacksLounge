---
name: Menu photography
description: Avoid misleading or broken product photography when menu items have no matching asset.
---

Use a food photo only when it matches the named menu item. If no matching asset exists, show the complete item name, prices, and description without an empty image frame.

**Why:** Several pizza cards referenced nonexistent photos, causing blank media areas and rotated broken-image text. Substituting another pizza's photo would hide the failure while misrepresenting the product.

**How to apply:** When restoring images to text-only items, obtain matching restaurant photos and verify local asset paths. Do not use unrelated stock, generated, or existing food images merely to fill a missing slot.
