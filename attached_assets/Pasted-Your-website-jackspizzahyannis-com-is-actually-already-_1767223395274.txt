Your website, jackspizzahyannis.com, is actually already doing a very good job with SEO basics. You have clear keywords in your titles and headers, and your image alt tags are descriptive.

However, there is always room to sharpen these to outrank local competitors and capture more specific "intent" (like people looking for delivery or late-night food).

Here is a breakdown of your current tags and my suggested improvements:

1. The Title Tag (The most important SEO tag)
The Title Tag is what appears in the Google search tab and as the blue link in search results.

Current: Best Pizza & Wings Hyannis MA | Jack's Lounge

The Problem: It's solid, but it misses your "secret sauce"—the Hot Honey. People search for unique items, and "Hot Honey Pizza" is a growing trend.

Improved: Jack's Lounge | Best Hot Honey Pizza & Wings in Hyannis, MA

Why: It leads with your brand name for recognition and includes your most unique menu item along with your high-volume keywords (Pizza, Wings, Hyannis).

2. The Meta Description
This is the "sales pitch" snippet that appears under your link in Google.

Current: (Varies, but currently focuses on a list of menu items).

The Problem: It’s a bit passive. It lists food but doesn't "sell" the experience or emphasize your 60-year history as strongly as it could.

Improved (155 characters): Taste the original Hot Honey Pizza at Jack's Lounge! Serving Hyannis since 1963 with the best wings, BBQ ribs & Italian favorites. Order online for fast delivery.

Why: It creates urgency ("Taste the original"), builds trust ("since 1963"), and highlights your delivery service, which is a major search factor.

3. Header Tags (H1, H2, H3)
These are the "headlines" on your actual webpage. Google uses them to understand the structure of your content.

Current H1: Best Pizza & Wings in Hyannis, MA - Jack's Lounge

Improved H1: Jack's Lounge: Home of the Original Hot Honey Pizza in Hyannis

Why: Your H1 should be unique. Using "Home of the Original" makes you the authority on that specific dish, while still keeping the "Hyannis" keyword.

Current H2 (Subheaders): You have good H2s like Best Wings in Hyannis.

Improvement Suggestion: Add a specific H2 for your location/vibe to capture "near me" searches:

Top-Rated Pizza & Pub Near Hyannis Harbor

Late-Night Pizza Delivery in Hyannis & Barnstable

4. Image "Alt" Tags
You are already doing this well, but you can add Location + Intent to them.

Current Style: Meat Lovers Pizza with Pepperoni...

Improved Style: Meat Lovers Pizza - Best Takeout Pizza Hyannis MA - Jack's Lounge

Why: If someone searches Google Images for "takeout pizza Hyannis," your photo is now much more likely to show up.

💡 The "Pro" Recommendation: Schema Markup
While not a "tag" you can see, Schema Markup is code you can add that tells Google exactly what your hours, price range, and menu are.

Does your website provider (or developer) use "Restaurant Schema"? If not, adding it would allow Google to show your 4.6-star rating and your price range ($) directly in the search results, which significantly increases clicks.
